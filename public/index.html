Your observation workspace

AERO-WATCH uses Sentinel-1 radar for forest change and NISAR L-band observations for flood tracking.

Choose your region

Search for a location, then use the drawing tool to select an area on the map. Imagery progress appears in the region panel.

Track new open water

In Floods, draw a region, enter start and end dates, and select Analyze floods. Red marks new open water relative to the first available observation. At least two NISAR acquisitions are required.

Explore changes over time

Drag the timeline to change dates. Play the sequence, adjust playback speed, or compare the current date with its baseline.
