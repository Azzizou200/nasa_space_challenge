NASA Space Apps Challenge: Universal SAR Forest Change Platform

Welcome to AERO-WATCH. This platform provides multi-temporal Earth observation monitoring across critical planetary ecosystems and extreme weather events utilizing all-weather Copernicus Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR).

Universal Square Region Selector

You can click the Square Selector Tool (or press [S]) and draw a bounding box anywhere on Earth. The pipeline calculates the geodetic surface area, assesses baseline canopy coverage, tracks illegal logging dynamics, and quantifies tree dissipation speed in km²/month.

Global Deforestation Hotspots:

  • Amazon Basin (Rondônia, Brazil): Arc of Deforestation multi-year SAR sequence.
  • Congo Basin (Mai-Ndombe, DR Congo): Africa's largest tropical carbon sink.
  • Southeast Asia (Borneo, Indonesia): Peatland swamp forest conversion to oil palm.
  • Gran Chaco (Paraguay): Rapid dry forest clearings for cattle ranching.
